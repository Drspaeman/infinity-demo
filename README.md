# Infinity (demo)

**Play it in your browser: https://drspaeman.github.io/infinity-demo/**

The first three floors of Infinity, a hardcore pixel-art climb up a tower that swallowed dying worlds.
Light three seals on each floor, read the boss's red warnings, and take the Stair up.
When your climber dies, the save is deleted. All you keep is what you learned.

- Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move, the mouse aims, left click attacks, right click or Shift rolls, E uses things, Esc pauses.
- A gamepad works too. Every key can be changed under Keys on the title screen.
- Best on a desktop browser. Your progress is kept in this browser only.
